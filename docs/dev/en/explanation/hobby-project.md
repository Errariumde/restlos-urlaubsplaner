# Hobby project

restlos is a hobby project without commitments. Its expectations are stated visibly in one place, in short, friendly and clear words: the project status notice in the [README](../../../../README.md#project-status). The GitHub settings that follow from it are in [GitHub settings](../reference/github-settings.md).

## Why

- **The expectations in public:** GitHub's guide on balance for maintainers advises exactly this, to make your own boundaries public and to say "no" openly ([opensource.guide](https://github.com/github/opensource.guide/blob/main/_articles/maintaining-balance-for-open-source-maintainers.md)).
- **A notice beside the license:** Art. 7 and 8 of the EUPL-1.2 already cover the legal side, no warranty and no liability; the notice covers the human side.
- **Wikis, Projects and Discussions off:** there is no channel for general questions ([`SUPPORT.md`](../../../../SUPPORT.md)).
- **Issues on and pull requests open to everyone:** contributions are welcome.
- **Squash merging only:** it keeps the history on `main` linear.
- **Moderation by hand:** the project is a hobby project without commitments. Issues are closed by hand, and interaction limits for new accounts are set only if needed.
- **A short code of conduct:** it gives a basis for closing unfriendly contributions.

## Rejected alternatives

- **A stale bot:** issues are closed by hand instead.
- **A channel for general questions:** Discussions stay off, and `SUPPORT.md` names the issue templates for bug reports and ideas.
