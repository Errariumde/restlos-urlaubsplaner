# Changelog

Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/), versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Specification, project files, Nix development environment, AI tooling configuration.
- Holiday and counting engine, with reading of plan files and rule sets.
- Main screen with the example plan: month tiles and month columns, planning by mouse, touch and keyboard, the allowance, the day detail, German and English, light and dark mode.
- Leave costs only where it overlaps the day's working time: on 24 December, whose afternoon is free, leave in the afternoon costs nothing, and leave in the morning frees the whole day for ½.
- The day detail's leave bar in the plan's smallest leave unit (at most 32 parts), with time without work, mandatory leave and other plans' leave as fixed blocks, one Tab stop, and tapping or dragging along it with the cost shown while dragging.
- A problem for own leave that lies on time that is off anyway.
